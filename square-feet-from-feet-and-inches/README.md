# How to calculate square footage from inches and fractions

Multiply the length in inches by the width in inches, then divide by 144, the number of square inches in a square foot: 48" × 96" is 4,608 square inches, or 32 square feet. With feet and inches, turn each side into inches first: 12' 6" is 150" and 10' 3" is 123", so the room is 18,450 square inches, or 128.13 square feet.

## Sizes measured in inches, in square feet

Data: [area-inches.csv](./area-inches.csv)

**How the numbers are worked out:** Square inches ÷ 144 = square feet, because a square foot is 12" × 12" = 144 square inches.

| Size (inches) | Square inches | Square feet | Square meters |
