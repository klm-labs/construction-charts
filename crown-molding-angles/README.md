# Crown molding angles chart for cutting crown flat on a miter saw

For a 90° corner, 52/38 crown cut flat on the saw needs a 31.6° miter and a 33.9° bevel; 45/45 crown needs a 35.3° miter and a 30° bevel. The chart gives both settings for wall corners from 60° to 150° in 5° steps, and inside and outside corners use the same numbers.

Web page: [https://klmlabs.app/construction-calculator/crown-molding-angles/](https://klmlabs.app/construction-calculator/crown-molding-angles/)  
Printable PDF (US Letter, one page): [crown-molding-angles.pdf](./crown-molding-angles.pdf)

## Crown molding miter and bevel settings, flat on the saw table

Data: [crown.csv](./crown.csv)

**How the numbers are worked out:** Miter = arctan(sin S ÷ tan(C ÷ 2)) and bevel = arcsin(cos S × cos(C ÷ 2)), where C is the wall corner and S the crown’s spring angle (38° for 52/38 crown, 45° for 45/45), to 0.1°.
