# What is a board foot, and how to calculate board feet

A board foot is a piece of lumber 12" long, 12" wide and 1" thick, or the same 144 cubic inches in any shape. To calculate board feet, multiply thickness × width in inches by the length in feet and divide by 12: a 2x4 that is 10 feet long is 2 × 4 × 10 ÷ 12 = 6.67 board feet.

## Board feet by nominal size and length

Data: [board-feet.csv](./board-feet.csv)

**How the numbers are worked out:** Board feet = thickness × width (inches) × length (inches) ÷ 144, on the nominal size (a 2x4 is counted as 2" × 4"), to 2 decimal places.

## Board feet per linear foot by thickness and width

Data: [board-feet-thickness.csv](./board-feet-thickness.csv)

**How the numbers are worked out:** Hardwood thickness is often given in quarters of an inch (4/4 = 1"). Construction Calculator counts stock thinner than 1" as 1" thick, which is why the 1/2" and 3/4" rows match 4/4.

| Thickness | 4" wide | 6" wide | 8" wide | 10" wide | 12" wide |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1/2" | 0.33 | 0.5 | 0.67 | 0.83 | 1 |
| 3/4" | 0.33 | 0.5 | 0.67 | 0.83 | 1 |
| 4/4 (1") | 0.33 | 0.5 | 0.67 | 0.83 | 1 |
| 5/4 (1-1/4") | 0.42 | 0.63 | 0.83 | 1.04 | 1.25 |
| 6/4 (1-1/2") | 0.5 | 0.75 | 1 | 1.25 | 1.5 |
| 8/4 (2") | 0.67 | 1 | 1.33 | 1.67 | 2 |

## Citations (as on the web page)

- Board foot: 12 in. long, 12 in. wide and 1 in. thick or its cubic equivalent; figured on nominal thickness and width and actual length; lumber under 1 in. nominal thickness is calculated as 1 in. Source: https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/62200.pdf (USDA Forest Products Laboratory, Wood Handbook FPL-GTR-282, 2021, glossary; read 2026-11-06)
- PS 20-20 section 2.2: board feet = nominal thickness in inches or fraction of an inch × nominal width in feet × length in feet; Table 3: a dry 2x4 is dressed to 1-1/2 in. × 3-1/2 in. Source: https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2019/12/11/PS%2020-20%20final%20WERB%20approved.pdf (NIST PS 20-20, American Softwood Lumber Standard; read 2026-11-06)
