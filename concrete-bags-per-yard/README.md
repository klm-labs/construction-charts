# How many bags of concrete in a yard: 80, 60 and 40 lb bags

It takes about 45 bags of 80 lb premixed concrete to make one cubic yard, 60 bags of 60 lb or 90 bags of 40 lb. That is 27 cubic feet divided by each bag's yield (0.60, 0.45 and 0.30 cubic feet on QUIKRETE's data sheet); with 10% extra for waste, buy 50, 66 or 99.

Web page: [https://klmlabs.app/construction-calculator/concrete-bags-per-yard/](https://klmlabs.app/construction-calculator/concrete-bags-per-yard/)  
Printable PDF (US Letter, one page): [concrete-bags-per-yard.pdf](./concrete-bags-per-yard.pdf)

## Bags of premixed concrete in one cubic yard

Data: [bags-per-yard.csv](./bags-per-yard.csv)

**How the numbers are worked out:** One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet. Bags = 27 ÷ the bag’s yield, rounded up to whole bags; the exact figure is in brackets when it isn’t whole.

| Bag | Yield per bag | Bags per cubic yard | With 10% waste |
|---|---|---|---|
| 80 lb | 0.6 cu ft | 45 | 50 (49.5) |
| 60 lb | 0.45 cu ft | 60 | 66 |
| 40 lb | 0.3 cu ft | 90 | 99 |
